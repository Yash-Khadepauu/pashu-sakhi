Para-vet Outbreak SOP

  • Isolate: Immediately segregate symptomatic cattle 50m away from healthy herd.
  • Restrict Movement: Prohibit movement of livestock in/out of the village for 14 days.
  • Do NOT Necropsy: In case of Anthrax suspicion, NEVER open carcass in the field.
  • Ring Vaccination: Mobilize vaccine stocks for a 5 km buffer radius around index case.
  • Bio-security: Disinfect stalls with 4% Sodium Carbonate or Potassium Permanganate.
