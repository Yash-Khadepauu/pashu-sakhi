Privacy Policy

PashuCare collects operational data — farmer, veterinarian and case records — solely to operate the livestock healthcare platform.

What we collect

Account details, case and treatment records, and platform usage logs needed to run the service.

How it's used

To assign cases, monitor animal health trends, and improve platform reliability. Data is never sold to third parties.

Your controls

You can review permission settings above and request account deletion at any time from the Account section.

Terms & Conditions

By using the PashuCare Admin Console you agree to use case, farmer and veterinarian data only for legitimate platform administration.

Acceptable use

Admin accounts must not be shared. Access to sensitive health records should be limited to operational need.

Availability

PashuCare is provided as-is during this prototype phase and features may change without notice.

FAQs

How do I assign a case to a veterinarian?

Open Cases from the sidebar and use the assign action on any unresolved case.

How do I verify a new veterinarian?

Go to Management → Veterinarians and approve pending registrations there.

Where can I export analytics data?

Use the Reports page, or the Export CSV action inside Analytics.

About PashuCare

PashuCare is a smart livestock healthcare platform connecting farmers with veterinarians, AI-assisted diagnosis, and 1962 emergency dispatch across rural India.

This Admin Console lets platform administrators monitor cases, manage farmer and veterinarian accounts, track disease trends, and keep the platform running smoothly.
